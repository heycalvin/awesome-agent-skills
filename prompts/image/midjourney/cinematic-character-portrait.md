# 电影质感叙事肖像 (Cinematic Character Portrait)

> **适用模型**：Midjourney v6.0 / v6.1  
> **画面风格**：35mm 电影胶片、伦勃朗光影、叙事感特写  
> **推荐比例**：`--ar 16:9` 或 `--ar 2:3`  

---

## 1. 提示词配方 (Prompt Formula)

### 完整提示词
```text
Cinematic film still, extreme close-up portrait of a weary 40-year-old female astrophysicist standing inside a dim observation deck, tired thoughtful eyes catching a faint reflection of the starry cosmos, subtle skin texture with visible pores and natural imperfections, illuminated by soft amber control panel glow and moody blue moonlight, shot on 35mm Arri Alexa camera, 85mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, delicate chromatic aberration, muted film color grading --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.1 --stylize 180
```

### 中文释义与结构拆解
- **主体**：`weary 40-year-old female astrophysicist`（神情疲惫且若有所思的 40 岁女性天文学家）
- **细节与情绪**：`tired thoughtful eyes catching a faint reflection of the starry cosmos, subtle skin texture with visible pores`（眼中倒映着微弱星光，极具真实的皮肤肌理与毛孔）
- **光影与氛围**：`soft amber control panel glow and moody blue moonlight`（琥珀色控制台微光与忧郁冷蓝月光的冷暖互补光）
- **摄影机质感**：`35mm Arri Alexa, 85mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field`（阿莱摄影机 85mm 变形宽银幕镜头，浅景深虚化）
- **参数控制**：
  - `--style raw`：降低 MJ 默认的过度插画化/油腻美化，呈现原生摄影质感。
  - `--stylize 180`：维持适度的艺术修饰，不过度失真。

---

## 2. 变换玩法 (Variations)

| 想要替换的风格 | 修改片段 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **赛博朋克风** | 将 `soft amber control panel glow` 替换为 `harsh neon magenta and cyan rim lights, wet raindrops glistening on skin` |
